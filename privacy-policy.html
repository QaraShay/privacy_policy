1. What StudyLock does

StudyLock lets you upload study materials, organises them by subject and topic, uses artificial intelligence to generate quizzes, explanations and tutor answers from your materials, and tracks your study progress and review schedule.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Data you provide

  • Account data: email address, display name and, if you sign up with email, a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google: a stable account identifier from the provider, and the email address and name it shares with us. Google may also share a link to your profile picture. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's relay address.
  • Study content: files you upload (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and text files), text you type or paste, links you add, the text extracted from all of these, and the subjects and topics you create.
  • Study activity: quiz questions and your answers, results, progress and review schedule, reward minutes, and your messages to the AI tutor together with its replies.
  • Settings: preferences such as daily goal, quiz length, difficulty and your notification preference.

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • Session data: when you sign in we record your device's user-agent string and IP address together with the session's creation and expiry time, to keep your account secure and prevent abuse.
  • Technical logs: our servers keep standard operational logs (for example request errors) for security and troubleshooting.

3.3 Data we do not collect

  • No advertising or analytics SDKs, and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell personal data.
  • No precise location, contacts, photo library, microphone, camera, health or payment data.
  • No push-notification tokens: this version does not send push notifications.
  • This version does not read or monitor which other apps you use.

4. Why we use your data

We use your data only to provide the Service:

  • to create and manage your account, sign you in and keep your data in sync;
  • to store and process your materials and generate quizzes, explanations and tutor answers from them;
  • to calculate your progress and schedule your reviews;
  • to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors;
  • to answer your requests and meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing, or training our own AI models, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal effects by automated means.

5. AI processing

To generate quizzes, tutor answers and embeddings (numerical representations used to search your materials), parts of your study content and your tutor messages are sent to OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) through its API. This includes:

  • excerpts of the text extracted from your materials;
  • images of pages from scanned PDFs, so the text in them can be recognised;
  • the questions you ask the AI tutor.

We send only what the request needs. We do not send your email address, name or password to OpenAI. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default, and OpenAI may keep it for a limited period (currently up to 30 days) to monitor for abuse. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

AI output can contain mistakes – check important facts against your original materials. Do not upload material you are not allowed to share, and avoid uploading documents containing sensitive personal data (yours or anyone else's), such as health, biometric or identity-document information.

8. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a local copy of your subjects, materials, quiz results and settings in the app's own storage on your device so that it works offline. Profile → Erase data on this device removes this local copy and signs you out. It does not delete your account on our servers.

9. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data for as long as your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored for it on our servers – profile, sign-in sessions, subjects, topics, materials and extracted text, embeddings, quizzes, answers, progress, tutor history, rewards and settings – and removes your uploaded files from storage. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days and in provider logs for a short period, after which they are deleted or overwritten.
  • If you signed in with Apple, you can also revoke StudyLock's access in your Apple Account settings on your device, under Sign in with Apple.
  • If you cannot access the app, email kalkabay.umar@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.
