1. What StudyLock does

StudyLock lets you upload study materials, organises them by subject and topic, uses artificial intelligence to extract text, generate quizzes, flashcards, explanations and tutor answers from your materials, schedules spaced-repetition reviews (FSRS), and tracks your study progress across devices.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you provide

  • Account data: email address, display name, internal user identifier (User ID) and, if you sign up with email and password, your password (stored exclusively as a salted bcrypt hash, never in plain text).
  • Sign in with Apple / Google: a stable account identifier (sub) from Apple or Google, plus the email address and display name shared by the provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive Apple's private relay address.
  • Study content: files and text you upload or paste (PDF, Word .docx, PNG/JPEG/WebP images, plain text), web links you add, the text extracted or normalised from them, vector embeddings generated from your text chunks, and the subjects and topics you create.
  • Study activity and state: generated quizzes and flashcards, your multiple-choice and open-ended answers, AI evaluation feedback, scores, spaced-repetition review state, study sessions, reward ledger minutes, and your messages to the AI tutor and its replies.
  • Settings: study preferences such as daily goal, quiz length, difficulty and notification preferences.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Authentication session metadata: when you sign in, we record a hashed refresh token, the session's creation and expiry timestamps, the device's User-Agent string and IP address to secure your account, manage active sessions and prevent abuse.
  • Technical logs: our servers record standard operational and error logs (such as request status and error diagnostics) for security, rate-limiting and troubleshooting.

2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app analytics SDKs, we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies (NSPrivacyTracking = false), we do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not collect precise geolocation, contacts, microphone, camera roll (beyond individual files you explicitly select for upload) or health data. This version of the app does not read or monitor which other apps you use on your device.

4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) processing

To provide StudyLock's core features, relevant parts of your study content and your interactions with the AI tutor are sent to OpenAI, L.L.C. through its API:

  • Text extraction (OCR) and formatting: when you upload scanned PDFs or images, the file is sent to OpenAI's Vision API to transcribe readable study text and format it into structured Markdown.
  • Semantic indexing (embeddings): text chunks from your materials are sent to OpenAI's Embeddings API to create numerical vectors stored in our database so the app can retrieve relevant passages.
  • Quizzes, flashcards, answer grading and AI tutor: relevant excerpts from your study materials, your open-ended quiz answers and your tutor chat messages are sent to OpenAI's API to generate questions, flashcards, grading feedback and tutor explanations.

We send only the content necessary to fulfil each request and configure OpenAI Responses API requests with store: false. Under OpenAI's API data usage policies, data submitted via the OpenAI API is not used to train or improve OpenAI's models by default; OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days solely to monitor for and prevent abuse before deletion. For details, see OpenAI's Enterprise & API Privacy documentation and OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

AI-generated outputs may contain inaccuracies. Please do not upload confidential material you do not have the right to share, and avoid uploading sensitive personal data (such as health, biometric, financial or government ID information).

The app stores a local copy of your study state (subjects, topics, materials metadata, quizzes, review schedules and settings) and authentication tokens in the device's sandboxed app storage so the app can load quickly and work offline. Selecting "Erase data on this device" in Profile clears this local cache and signs you out on that device without deleting your cloud account.
