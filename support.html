Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I upload study materials?

Tap on any Subject, open a Topic (or create a new one), and tap Add Material. You can upload PDFs, Word documents, images, or paste text and links. Our AI will automatically extract readable text and prepare quizzes for you.

Why does a newly uploaded material take a moment to be ready?

Our background service extracts the text, creates searchable semantic embeddings, and generates a starter quiz. For large PDFs or scanned documents, this processing typically takes between 10 to 60 seconds.

How can I delete my account and data?

You can permanently delete your account and all associated study materials at any time directly in the app. Go to Profile → Delete account. Once confirmed, all your materials, progress, and account data are immediately and permanently erased from our servers.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please send us an email at the support address above with your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue or idea.
